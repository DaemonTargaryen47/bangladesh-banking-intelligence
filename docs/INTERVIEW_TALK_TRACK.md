# Interview Talk Track

## 30-second version
I built a Bangladesh-focused banking intelligence case study to show how I approach management decisions with data. I analyzed six years of synthetic bank financials across growth, profitability, asset quality, capital, efficiency and digital adoption. I used SQL for peer and trend analysis, Python for EDA, and designed a Power BI executive dashboard with DAX measures. The key part was not the visuals—it was converting the KPIs into actions around credit quality, funding mix, capital discipline and operating efficiency.

## If asked why synthetic data
I wanted the project to be fully reproducible without misrepresenting unaudited or inconsistently extracted bank figures. The data model is designed so official annual-report or Bangladesh Bank figures can replace the synthetic dataset without rebuilding the analytical layer.

## Questions you should be ready for
- Why can rapid loan growth be risky?
- Why is a lower NPL ratio important?
- What does ROA tell management that ROE does not?
- Why does CASA matter to a bank?
- Why can a high loan-to-deposit ratio create pressure?
- What actions could improve cost-to-income?
- What tradeoff exists between growth and capital adequacy?
