# Business Case

## Management question
Which banks appear strongest on growth, profitability, risk and efficiency, and where should management focus next?

## Analytical framework
1. **Growth:** assets, deposits, loans and profit
2. **Profitability:** ROA, ROE and net profit
3. **Asset quality:** NPL ratio
4. **Capital:** capital adequacy
5. **Efficiency:** cost-to-income and profit per employee
6. **Funding:** CASA and loan-to-deposit
7. **Distribution:** branches and digital users

## Decisions supported
- Where rapid growth may be creating credit-quality pressure
- Which peers generate superior returns from assets/equity
- Which banks carry inefficient operating structures
- Which banks may benefit from stronger low-cost deposit acquisition
- How digital adoption compares with physical distribution growth

## Management recommendations
- Tighten underwriting and collections when NPL growth outpaces loan growth.
- Protect capital buffers when risk-weighted growth accelerates.
- Improve cost-to-income through process automation and channel migration.
- Increase CASA mix through salary accounts, transaction banking and digital engagement.
- Use digital adoption per branch as an efficiency lens before adding physical locations.
